Screend is the family media tracker that helps parents review movies, shows, and games — and gives kids a voice in the process. Know what they're watching. Make it a conversation.

🎬

Kids Request

Let your kids submit titles for review. No more "can I watch this?" interruptions at dinner.

🤖

AI Review

AI reviews requested media using criteria you set up yourself so it fits your family. Decisions and notes get saved automatically.

📋

Decision Tracker

Track every movie, show, and video game your family has reviewed so you don't have to remember. Easily sort in a variety of ways.

⚖️

Appeals

Kids can appeal a decision. It teaches them to advocate for themselves — respectfully.

🔒

Age-Gating

Set different verdicts for different kids. A PG-13 film might be fine for your 15-year-old but not your 8-year-old. Each kid only sees what's meant for them.

👫

Two Parents

Invite a second parent or guardian. They get full access to reviews, the inbox, and settings — same as you. No more being the only one managing it.
