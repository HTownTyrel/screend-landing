Delete Your Account & Data

How to delete your Skopd account — or just specific data — and exactly what is removed.

Delete from inside the app

The fastest way to delete your Skopd account is directly in the app:

  1. Open Skopd and sign in.
  2. Go to the menu → Settings.
  3. Scroll to the Delete Account section at the bottom.
  4. Tap Delete My Account & Family — or Delete My Account if you're a second parent.
  5. Type DELETE to confirm, and submit.

If you are the family owner, this deletes the entire family account (every parent and child). If you are a second parent, it removes only your own login and leaves the family — owned by the primary parent — intact.

Delete specific data (keep your account)

You don't have to delete your whole account to remove data. Inside the app you can:

  • Delete a review — open it in your review log and remove it.
  • Remove a child — Settings → Kids → tap the ✕ next to a child to delete that child and all of their data.
  • Delete a request or appeal — remove it from your inbox.

Prefer email? Contact hello@getskopd.com from your account email and tell us exactly what you'd like removed.

What gets deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently remove all of the following from our active systems:

  • Your login and email address (and your co-parent's, if you're the family owner)
  • Your parent profile and name
  • Every child profile — names, ages, and PINs
  • All reviews, verdicts, review requests, and appeals
  • Any subscription records and in-app purchase history we store
  • Push-notification tokens and device identifiers
  • Internal usage logs and pending invites tied to your family

In short: everything associated with your account is deleted. We do not keep a personal copy of your data after deletion.

Data retention

Your data is removed from our active systems immediately when you delete your account. Any residual copies that remain in our routine, encrypted database backups are automatically purged within 30 days, after which no copy of your data remains.

We do not sell your data, and we do not retain personal information after deletion for any other purpose.
